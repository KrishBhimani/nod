# Apps cards — 2026-09-26

The revised card layout is now part of the real Apps page. Connected services and available services use separate grids. Short unconnected cards keep Connect on the right. Connected cards show a compact Add account action and plain account rows with an account-specific × control. Notion rows retain workspace names when several workspaces share an owner email.

Disconnect opens a confirmation with Keep account focused first. Accepted removal updates the cache and returns focus to the service action. Failed requests keep the account and dialog available for retry. Duplicate requests are blocked, leaving Apps aborts the pending request, and late reads cannot restore a removed account.
